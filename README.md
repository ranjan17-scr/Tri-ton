# Tri-ton

# Problem statement :
While general awareness of climate change is high, everyday consumers face significant friction in adopting sustainable energy behaviours. There is a disconnect between macro-level climate goals and micro-level daily habits. Citizens lack personalised, actionable tools to measure their individual carbon footprints, provide real-time energy-reduction suggestions, and visualise the tangible impact of their actions.

# Features:
- **Personalised Footprint Tracker:** A streamlined onboarding flow that calculates a user's baseline energy or carbon footprint and visualises their improvement over time.
- **Behavioural Nudges & Micro-Tasks:** A system that delivers contextual, actionable alerts and daily sustainability challenges.
- **Gamification & Social Impact:** A community-driven mechanism, such as leaderboards, localised community goals, or a points-based reward system that validates and encourages green actions.
- **Actionable Resource Hub:** Automated calculator for rooftop solar feasibility and a directory for sustainable energy vendors.

# Tech Stack :
- **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS, Javascript.
- **Backend:** Node.js.
- **Database:** MongoDB.
- **APIs & Integrations:** OpenWeather API, Maps API.
